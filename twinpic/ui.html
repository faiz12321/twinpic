TwinPic

Find photos that look the same. Keep everything safe.

Looking never changes anything. You decide what happens next.
Nothing is deleted. The holding folder keeps every extra copy, and Undo puts them all back.
This restores photos from an earlier move, even from a previous TwinPic session.
TwinPic finds the same picture in different files: copies, resized and re-compressed versions. It may not catch edited photos (brightened, cropped, filtered), and pictures that only look alike can sometimes pair up - glance at the groups before moving. Your photos never leave this computer.
